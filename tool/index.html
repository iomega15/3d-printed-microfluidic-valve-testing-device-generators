Inputs (what you set)
Outputs (what the tool returns)
g — open lumen: flow gap left under the fully deflected membrane (= s − d)
C — required membrane width (width of the deformable membrane region)
κ — membrane deflection coefficient at the operating pressure: sagitta per unit membrane width (dimensionless, s/C in matching units). Stiffer / thicker membranes have smaller κ.
R — radius of curvature of the deflected membrane
W — flow-channel width
s — sagitta: peak membrane deflection (chord → arc bottom)
Hd — doormat / seat height (set 0 for a recess cut into the floor)
d — depth the seat cuts below the channel ceiling (= s − g)
n — deflection exponent (n = 1: linear; n = 4/3 ≈ large-deflection membrane theory)
Hc — total channel height (= Hd + g)
Note: fit κ only on the pre-contact (rising) regime of your deflection data — once the membrane touches the channel floor, measured reach saturates at the channel height and no longer reflects free deflection; including saturated widths underestimates κ.
